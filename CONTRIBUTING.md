# Wheat: basic observation guidance

This is starter educational content, not a complete agronomic recommendation.
Before suggesting a treatment, collect the crop growth stage, symptom distribution,
soil moisture, recent weather, and any soil-test or fertiliser history.

Yellowing can have multiple causes, including nutrient issues, waterlogging, root
problems, or disease. Observe whether older or newer leaves are affected and whether
symptoms occur across the field or in patches. Avoid recommending exact fertiliser
or pesticide doses without locally applicable verified guidance.

For region-specific recommendations, consult current guidance from an agricultural
university, KVK, or qualified local agricultural officer. Add source URL, publication
date, crop stage, and regional applicability when expanding this knowledge file.
